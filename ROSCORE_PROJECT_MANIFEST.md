# Rosscore Project Manifest — Thabz Driving School

## Product thesis
A lightweight lead-generation website for a South African driving school. Primary conversion: private WhatsApp enquiry. Secondary conversion: phone call.

## Audience and job
Prospective learner and licence applicants need to understand training categories, trust the business enough to contact it, and get a private quote without a public price list.

## Conversion path
Landing page → understand training categories → choose an enquiry → WhatsApp or call → discuss current pricing and package details privately.

## Commercial constraints
- Pricing stays private; public copy uses enquiry prompts only.
- No unsupported claims, fake social proof, or unconfirmed package inclusions.
- Commercial budget details are not stored in public project files.
- Owner must confirm contact details, prices/inclusions, service area, arrangements, and brand imagery.

## Technical baseline
Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript, GitHub Pages, mobile-first and accessible, minimal dependencies, versioned local assets, no unnecessary analytics.

## Release checks
Metadata and CTA destinations; no public prices; local assets resolve; responsive/accessibility checks; deployment workflow and permissions; post-commit health artifact.

## Known limitations
The app-source image mentioned by the owner was not available as a readable file in this execution context. Do not claim integration until the real asset is located and verified. Browser/device rendering has not been verified. Business facts require owner confirmation.
